# Meeting minutes

Type: `minutes`. Usual mode: `argument`.

Distinguish decisions, proposals and unresolved items. Retain stated owners and deadlines; leave absent ones unknown.

Keep speaker labels, action lists and repeated endings.

Do not turn a suggestion into a decision or fill an unassigned action with a guessed owner.

Stylistic checker candidates omitted: `label-colon`, `uniform-endings`, `uniform-length`, `bullet-ratio`.

An omission is not a pass verdict. Review unclear meaning in context.
