# Proposals

Type: `proposal`. Usual mode: `argument`.

Explain the proposed action, why it addresses the stated problem and what changes for the reader. Preserve assumptions and exclusions.

Keep alternative lists, comparison tables and a clear recommendation supported by the material.

Do not invent savings or numerical benefits. A concrete removed step can explain a benefit without a measured percentage.

Stylistic checker candidates omitted: none.

An omission is not a pass verdict. Review unclear meaning in context.
