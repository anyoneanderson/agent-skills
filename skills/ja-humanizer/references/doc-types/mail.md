# Mail and chat

Type: `mail`. Usual mode: `mail`.

State the purpose and requested action in the sender's usual politeness level. Keep necessary prerequisites and what the recipient needs to respond.

Keep greetings, closing, repeated polite endings and courteous conditional requests that express real consent or permission.

Do not shorten away the sender's consideration or invent a deadline or promise.

Stylistic checker candidates omitted: `uniform-endings`.
