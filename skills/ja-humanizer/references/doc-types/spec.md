# Specifications and designs

Type: `spec`. Usual mode: `argument`.

Name the trigger, actor, input, operation and result; preserve conditions, exceptions and permission boundaries.

Keep requirement lists, repeated normative endings and established technical terms.

Do not soften obligations or invent actors, guarantees or a cause to make a sentence concrete.

Stylistic checker candidates omitted: `label-colon`, `uniform-endings`, `uniform-length`, `bullet-ratio`.

An omission is not a pass verdict. Review unclear meaning in context.
