# Slides

Type: `slides`. Usual mode: `argument / narrative`.

Write for the supplied slide structure and audience. Keep each item understandable without adding a slogan.

Keep short phrases, nominal headings, bullets and intentional fragments. Do not apply the prose rhythm target to slide fragments.

Do not delete a condition or exaggerate certainty to fit a short line.

Stylistic checker candidates omitted: `label-colon`, `uniform-endings`, `uniform-length`, `bullet-ratio`, `copy-fragments`, `heading-sentence`, `rhythm-mismatch`.

An omission is not a pass verdict. Review unclear meaning in context.
