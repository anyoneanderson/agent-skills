# Articles and personal writing

Type: `article`. Usual mode: `argument / narrative`.

Follow the requested audience and purpose. Use narrative mode for supplied experiences; use argument for explanations. The explainer subtype remains explicit-only.

Keep the writer's natural long sentences, asides, meaningful metaphors and an editorially required summary.

Do not manufacture personal anecdotes or broaden one example into a general law.

Stylistic checker candidates omitted: none.

An omission is not a pass verdict. Review unclear meaning in context.
