# Incident reports

Type: `incident`. Usual mode: `argument`.

Separate the observed symptom, confirmed cause, hypothesis and action already taken. Tie each countermeasure to the relevant operation.

Keep timestamps, uncertainty and a chronological list when needed.

Do not turn silent failure into missing logs without evidence or promise prevention that the source does not support.

Stylistic checker candidates omitted: none.

An omission is not a pass verdict. Review unclear meaning in context.
